          Essential Safety Guidelines

          Protect yourself and your loved ones from digital fraud.

          "Never share your OTP, password, PIN or banking credentials with someone just because a message asks for them."

          "Always verify important information through official channels."

          1. Keep Credentials Private

          Banks and legitimate companies will never request your OTP, PIN, or passwords over chat or phone calls.

          2. Cross-Verify Channels

          When in doubt, open the official app or website directly by typing the known URL in your browser.

          3. Never Pay for Jobs

          Legitimate employers do not ask candidates for registration fees or advance security deposits.

          4. Pause Under Pressure

          Scammers rely on fake urgency. Take a breath and consult a trusted person before taking action.

          🔒 Privacy & Responsible Use

          Use caution when sharing sensitive information. ScamCheck AI is designed to provide risk analysis, not to replace official verification.

          Avoid pasting sensitive personally identifiable information such as complete passwords, private PINs, or confidential identity documents into any web tool. Always confirm urgent financial matters with your authorized financial provider directly.
